# Clip style guide (the user's rules — follow on every clip)

## Picking moments
- Punchy, useful moments: self-improvement tips, founder lessons, wild true stories. Not slow life-story setup.
- One continuous thought per clip, in the order it was said. Never stitch unrelated numbers/stats together.
- Open on the strongest thing: a big number, a big moment, a bold line. No build-up, no fluff.
- Length 15–35 s.

## Hook (on-screen text)
- At the TOP, just below the app's top bar (top edge ~255 px of 1920) — never in the middle, never higher.
- Must never cover the face: during the hook the shot sits lower over a blurred scenery backdrop, then glides up.
- Big words that make strangers stop: "CEO", "$3.4B", "$100M", numbers, stakes. People don't know who the guest is —
  tell them why he matters ("$3.4B CEO REVEALS…").
- Big, colourful boxes (white / yellow / red), 3D emoji, readable from frame 1.

## Captions
- Big, near the centre of the screen, 1–2 words at a time, active word in a coloured box with a bounce.
- Key words yellow. Swear words starred. Captions = exactly what is audible; proofread names.
- Never two captions on screen at once.

## Editing
- Never clip a word: cut only in real audio pauses (energy-based), keep breathing room.
- First frame: impact sound + zoom punch. Music starts on its energetic part.
- Minimal zoom (sharpness matters). Music audible but under the voice (~-21 LUFS bed).

## B-roll
- Plenty: mix full-screen cutaways and pop-in cards, each on the exact word it illustrates.
- Never overlapping the hook.
